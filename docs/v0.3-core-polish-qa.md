## Final candidate quality pass

The six B surfaces received another direct rendered review. Small presentation changes align mixed collection-card text baselines, put hotspot product information before its action and give the cart-drawer note and secondary actions a tighter rhythm. There was no reason to alter the Before/After frame or Bento layout after reviewing the rendered states. The 18-surface grades remain **9 A / 9 B / 0 C / 0 D**; no grade was promoted based on these small refinements.

Screenshots for Collection Cards, Bento, Shoppable Image, Before/After, Cart Drawer and Footer were captured at actual viewport widths 390, 768 and 1440. Responsive overflow checks at 320, 375, 430 and 1024 passed. Direct rendered content used existing QA-store assets only. Evidence is in `../artifacts/nami-rc-final/` relative to the workspace, including `visual.json`, screenshots and runtime summaries.

Fresh Theme Editor review confirmed Hero spacing controls and mobile preview; temporary Group creation and nested content removal/undo; and an already installed Sign in with Shop app block rendering, reordering, removal, and removal of its temporary section. Nothing was saved. This verifies one available app block only, not arbitrary third-party app compatibility.

Liquid inspection over all 14 QA products found zero native Shopify swatch metadata. The Videographer product has one external video and no Shopify-hosted video; no product has `model` media. Accordingly native swatch rendering, Shopify-hosted video sizing and 3D model rendering remain unverified because the store contains no genuine fixture. No catalog fixture was invented.

WebKit and Firefox smoke passed home, product variant switching, cart quantity/removal and Checkout visibility at 390, 768 and 1440; mobile navigation open/Escape/focus return passed at 390. Firefox reported no page errors. WebKit emitted three cross-origin script errors per viewport for Shopify's redirected `origin_trials` script; neither browser reported failed Nami theme assets. Shopify pixel, analytics and telemetry requests also failed or aborted in both engines and are platform noise, not theme failures. Physical-device testing remains outstanding.

The workflow now pins `ubuntu-24.04`, `actions/checkout@v7` and `actions/setup-node@v7` to avoid the prior runner migration and Node 20 deprecation warnings. The versions resolve to v7.0.1 and v7.0.0 in the official action repositories; warning removal is not confirmed until the new workflow run.

Final local validation: Shopify Theme Check, 85 files and zero offenses; `node scripts/validate-theme.cjs`, 73 documents; hardening, v0.2 upgrade, P0/P1, P2 and polish suites; both JavaScript syntax checks; and `git diff --check` passed. The Shopify Liquid skill's separate validator could not start because its bundled environment lacks `@shopify/theme-check-common`; Theme Check and repository validation provide the available validation. The strict final upload and final CI result are recorded in the release-candidate handoff after they run.

Estimated merchant-quality readiness: approximately **80%**, a judgement rather than a measured completion percentage. Remaining B grades are not release-blocking regressions, but this pass does not authorize release.
